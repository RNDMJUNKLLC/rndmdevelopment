To see reCAPTCHA in action, you need to configure it:

Step 2: Create a new site with:

  • Domain: localhost (for development)
Step 3: Copy your site key and update src/firebase-config.js:
siteKey: "YOUR_ACTUAL_SITE_KEY_HERE"
Step 4: Refresh this page to see reCAPTCHA in action!
